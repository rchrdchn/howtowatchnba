## Who reads How to Watch NBA

- **Local fans** whose team changed channels this season and want the answer fast.
- **Cord-cutters** comparing DAZN, team apps, Peacock, Prime Video and antennas.
- **Out-of-market fans** sorting out League Pass and blackouts.

Visitors arrive with one question: where is the game on, and what does it cost? That is purchase intent for streaming, antennas, tickets and anything else a game-night fan needs.

## What the site covers

- 30 team pages with hand-checked local channels, streaming options, free games and sources.
- A tonight grid with every game by channel, refreshed nightly.
- A ZIP blackout checker and a tracker of where every team moved.
- A free weekly newsletter with each fan's games and channels.

## Placements

### Newsletter sponsor

One sponsor per issue, at the top of the weekly email. Your logo, a short message (up to about 60 words) and one link, clearly marked as sponsored.

### Banner on team pages

One sponsor per team page, per conference, or site-wide. A lightweight image or text banner, clearly labeled. No pop-ups, no autoplay and no third-party ad trackers.

### Tonight grid sponsor

A "presented by" line on the tonight grid on the homepage and the tonight page.

## Launch pricing

The site launched in October 2026, so we don't quote traffic numbers yet. Early sponsors get launch rates. Ask and we'll share current numbers before you book, plus a click report after your run.

## Our rules

- **Legal only.** Licensed broadcasters, official retailers and legitimate businesses. No unlicensed streams or IPTV resellers.
- **Always labeled.** Every paid placement is marked as sponsored, and links use rel="sponsored".
- **Editorial stays independent.** Sponsors never change what a page says about channels, prices or blackouts.
