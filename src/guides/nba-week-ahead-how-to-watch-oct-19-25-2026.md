## Will League Pass black out these games?

Yes, when you are in a team's home market and the game is on that team's local channel or on NBA TV.

National exclusives on NBC, Peacock, ESPN, ABC and Prime Video are **not** League Pass games. Watch them on the national service instead.

Use our [blackout checker](/blackouts/) for a ZIP estimate. The [NBA's own League Pass blackout guide](https://support.watch.nba.com/hc/en-us/articles/115002481154-League-Pass-Blackout-Guide) is the final word.
