**Peacock NBA Monday games stream on Peacock and air on NBCSN, and the first one is Warriors at Nuggets on Monday Oct 26 at 10pm ET (7pm PT).** The cheapest way in is Peacock Premium at $12.99 a month; NBCSN only comes with YouTube TV, Fubo or Xfinity, and your local NBC station does not carry these games.

NBC calls these games exclusive to Peacock and NBCSN, so the teams' local channels go dark and League Pass blacks them out. For every other game on a given night, see [Tonight's games](/tonight/).

## When is the first Peacock NBA Monday game?

| Date | Game | ET / PT | Where to watch | Local channel that night |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Mon Oct 26 | [Warriors](/teams/warriors/) @ [Nuggets](/teams/nuggets/) | 10pm / 7pm | Peacock, NBCSN | None. NBCS Bay Area and Altitude do not air it. |

The call is Michael Grady on play-by-play with Robbie Hummel (Warriors side) and Austin Rivers (Nuggets side) in NBC's "On the Bench" format. Ahmed Fareed hosts the studio with Carmelo Anthony, Vince Carter and Tracy McGrady, per NBC Sports' Oct 14 assignments.

Why watch (opinion): Stephen Curry and Nikola Jokic in a late West Coast window, and both teams are still looking for an early statement. As of Thursday morning ESPN had the Warriors at 0-1 after Wednesday's loss at the Lakers, and the Nuggets at 0-0 before tonight's ESPN game in Oklahoma City.

## Can I watch Peacock NBA Monday with an antenna?

No. NBCSN is a cable channel and Peacock is a streaming app, so an antenna will not pick up Monday games. NBC's over-the-air stations carry the Tuesday games, not Mondays.

If your team has a free antenna home after FanDuel Sports Network, that still covers its local games on other nights. See [Where Every Team Moved](/moved/).

## Why can't I see Warriors at Nuggets on League Pass?

Peacock and NBCSN games are national exclusives, like ESPN, ABC, NBC and Prime Video games. League Pass blacks them out in every market, not just Denver and the Bay Area. The same game is on Peacock, so that is the workaround.

For regular local games, League Pass blackouts depend on your ZIP code. Check yours with our [blackout checker](/blackouts/); the [NBA's League Pass blackout guide](https://support.watch.nba.com/hc/en-us/articles/115002481154-League-Pass-Blackout-Guide) has the final word.

## How is Peacock NBA Monday different from Coast 2 Coast Tuesday?

Tuesday games are on NBC stations and Peacock, not NBCSN. Coast 2 Coast Tuesday starts the next night, Oct 27.

| Game | ET / PT | Where to watch |
|---|---|---|
| Pistons @ Knicks | 8pm / 5pm | NBC (East and Central stations), Peacock |
| [Trail Blazers](/teams/trail-blazers/) @ Lakers | 11pm / 8pm | NBC (Pacific and Mountain stations), Peacock |

NBC says that on most Tuesdays the 8pm ET game goes to NBC stations in the Eastern and Central time zones and the 8pm PT game goes to Pacific and Mountain stations, while both stream nationwide on Peacock. So a West Coast fan who wants Pistons at Knicks needs Peacock, and an East Coast fan staying up for the Lakers needs it too. ESPN's listings also show Spectrum SportsNet for the Lakers game; that is **unconfirmed**, so plan on NBC or Peacock.
