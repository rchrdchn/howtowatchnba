## How can I stream tonight's games?

**NBA TV games:** on NBA TV through cable and live TV streaming services that carry it, and on both teams' local channels and streams in their home markets.

**Local games:** each team's own stream works in its home market (Local 10+ Platinum, Hornets on DAZN, YES on DAZN, Cavs on DAZN, Bucks+, Monumental+, the CHSN app, Spurs on DAZN, MavsTV, SCHN+, Pelicans+, Altitude+ and Suns+, among others). Out of market, all eight games are on NBA League Pass, $16.99 a month or $109.99 for the season (checked October 18, 2026).

## Will tonight's games be blacked out?

There are no national exclusives tonight. NBA TV games still air on the local channels, and the NBA's League Pass blackout guide lists only ESPN, ABC, NBC, Peacock and Prime Video as national blackouts.

League Pass blacks out each game only inside either team's home market, where it airs on the local channel instead. Check your ZIP code on the [League Pass blackout checker](/blackouts/).
