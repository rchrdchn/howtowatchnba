## How can I stream tonight's games?

**ESPN games:** the ESPN app with ESPN Unlimited, $31.99 a month or $319.99 a year (checked October 17, 2026), or any cable or live TV service with ESPN.

**Local games:** each team's own stream works in its home market. Out of market, every local game tonight is on NBA League Pass.

Want the season total for your team? Try the [cheapest way to watch your team](/tools/cheapest-way-to-watch-your-team/) calculator.

## Will tonight's games be blacked out?

The two ESPN games are blacked out live on League Pass everywhere in the US, and the teams' local channels don't carry them.

For the other nine, League Pass blacks out the game only inside either team's home market, where it airs on the local channel instead. Check your ZIP code on the [League Pass blackout checker](/blackouts/).

## Hawks at Magic: 7pm ET (4pm PT)

**Where to watch:** [Hawks](/teams/hawks/) on WANF Atlanta News First (46), free over the air; their local streaming option is **unconfirmed**. [Magic](/teams/magic/) on MagicTV 27, free over the air, and Magic on DAZN. Kia Center, Orlando.

**Why watch:** two Southeast rivals who both finished above .500 last season (Hawks 46-36, Magic 45-37), and both teams' local games are free on antenna TV after leaving FanDuel Sports Network.

**Injury report (10am ET):** Orlando has Anthony Black, Jonathan Isaac, Izaiyah Nelson and Noah Penda out. Atlanta has Mouhamed Gueye and Henri Veesaar out.

## Bucks at Wizards: 7pm ET (4pm PT)

**Where to watch:** [Bucks](/teams/bucks/) on My24, free over the air, and Bucks+. [Wizards](/teams/wizards/) on Monumental Sports Network and Monumental+. Capital One Arena, Washington.

**Why watch:** both teams are trying to climb out of last season's lottery (Wizards 17-65, Bucks 32-50).

**Injury report (10am ET):** Washington has AJ Dybantsa (left ankle), Tristan Vukcevic and Jamir Watkins out. Milwaukee's Nate Ament is probable.

## Timberwolves at Heat: 7:30pm ET (4:30pm PT), ESPN

**Where to watch:** ESPN and the ESPN app. Kaseya Center, Miami.

**Local homes when not national:** the [Timberwolves](/teams/timberwolves/) are on DAZN, with 15 games free on KARE 11. The [Heat](/teams/heat/) moved to WPLG Local 10, free over the air.

**Why watch:** two new star pairings meet for the first time. Minnesota added LaMelo Ball to go with Anthony Edwards, and Miami brought in Giannis Antetokounmpo to pair with Bam Adebayo (NBA.com).

**Injury report (10am ET):** Minnesota's Donte DiVincenzo is out. Miami's Vladislav Goldin is questionable.

## Hornets at Nets: 7:30pm ET (4:30pm PT)

**Where to watch:** [Hornets](/teams/hornets/) on WSOC 9 and TV64, free over the air, and Hornets on DAZN. [Nets](/teams/nets/) on YES Network, with YES on DAZN in market. Barclays Center, Brooklyn.

**Why watch:** Charlotte went 44-38 last season and Brooklyn went 20-62, but the Hornets are short-handed to start.

**Injury report (10am ET):** Charlotte has Kylan Boswell, Kon Knueppel, Coby White and Grant Williams out. Brooklyn's Tyler Bilodeau is out.

## Bulls at Raptors: 7:30pm ET (4:30pm PT)

**Where to watch:** [Bulls](/teams/bulls/) on Chicago Sports Network and the CHSN app. The [Raptors](/teams/raptors/) have no US home market, so US fans outside Chicago watch on League Pass. Scotiabank Arena, Toronto.

**Why watch:** Toronto went 46-36 last season and opens at home against a Bulls team that went 31-51.

**Injury report (10am ET):** Chicago has Nic Claxton out and Jordan Hawkins and Dailyn Swain doubtful (illness). Toronto's Nate Bittle is out.

## Warriors at Lakers: 10pm ET (7pm PT), ESPN

**Where to watch:** ESPN and the ESPN app. Crypto.com Arena, Los Angeles.

**Local homes when not national:** the [Warriors](/teams/warriors/) are on NBC Sports Bay Area. The [Lakers](/teams/lakers/) are on Spectrum SportsNet.

**Why watch:** it's the Lakers' first game since LeBron James left. Luka Doncic, Austin Reaves and newcomer Walker Kessler face Stephen Curry's Warriors (NBA.com, USA Today).

**Injury report (10am ET):** Golden State has Jimmy Butler III, Moses Moody, Brandin Podziemski and Kristaps Porzingis (not with team) out; Curry is probable with right ankle soreness. The Lakers have Cameron Carr and Adou Thiero out and Collin Sexton questionable.

## Suns at Trail Blazers: 10pm ET (7pm PT)

**Where to watch:** [Suns](/teams/suns/) on Arizona's Family 3TV, free over the air, and Suns+. [Trail Blazers](/teams/trail-blazers/) on KUNP and the Rip City Television Network, free over the air, and BlazerVision. Moda Center, Portland.

**Injury report (10am ET):** Portland has Shaedon Sharpe, Branden Carlson and Chris Youngblood out, with Deni Avdija questionable. Phoenix has Mark Williams out and Luke Kennard questionable.

## Kings at Clippers: 10:30pm ET (7:30pm PT)

**Where to watch:** [Kings](/teams/kings/) on NBC Sports California, with its Peacock regional add-on. [Clippers](/teams/clippers/) on ClipperVision, in the NBA app. Intuit Dome, Inglewood.

**Why watch:** the late game on the board, and both teams are missing key players to start. The Clippers went 42-40 last season and the Kings 22-60.

**Injury report (10am ET):** the Clippers have Bradley Beal, Brandon Ingram, Jordan Miller, Yanic Konan Niederhauser and Max Strus out. Sacramento has Ben Simmons, Dylan Cardwell and Maxime Raynaud out, with Malik Monk questionable.
