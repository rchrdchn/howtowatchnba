## Bulls 132, Bucks 125

**By quarter:** Chicago 17-44-41-30, Milwaukee 32-30-42-21. Chicago trailed 32-17 after one, then scored 44 in the second quarter.

**Top performers:**
- Tyler Herro (MIL): 34 points, 12-24 FG, 5-10 3PT
- Norman Powell (CHI): 25 points, 7-20 FG, 9-9 FT
- Caleb Wilson (CHI): 21 points, 7-14 FG, 6 rebounds
- Jaime Jaquez Jr. (MIL): 19 points, 11 rebounds, 5 assists
- Leonard Miller (CHI): 19 points off the bench, 7-11 FG
- Jalen Smith (CHI): 14 points, 13 rebounds
- Myles Turner (MIL): 12 points, 5 blocks

**Key moments:** the game tipped at 4pm ET, four hours earlier than first scheduled, because the Brewers hosted Game 1 of the World Series that night (AP). The teams traded leads before Chicago closed it at the free-throw line, going 28 for 30 as a team to Milwaukee's 14 for 26. The Bucks hit 19 3s and still fell to 0-2. Herro, a Milwaukee-area native, led all scorers in his Fiserv Forum debut, and the Bucks honored the late Don Nelson with a moment of silence and a halftime tribute.

**Injuries:** none reported in the AP game story.

**Up next:** Bulls host the Wizards tonight. Bucks visit the 76ers tonight on NBA TV.

**Top performers:**
- Jayson Tatum (BOS): 36 points, 14-26 FG, 9 rebounds, 7 assists, 3 steals
- Karl-Anthony Towns (NY): 22 points, 9-13 FG, 10 rebounds
- OG Anunoby (NY): 20 points, 10 rebounds, 8 assists

- Jalen Brunson (NY): 19 points, 7-17 FG
- Paul George (BOS): 15 points, 7 rebounds
- Neemias Queta (BOS): 15 points, 6 rebounds, 3 blocks

**Key moments:** Boston led 91-79 before a 12-3 Knicks run made it 94-91 on a Towns putback dunk. Tatum scored seven of the Celtics' final eight points: a 19-footer for 101-98 with 1:11 left, then a 3 from the wing in the final minute. He had 25 of his 36 after halftime. Mikal Bridges went 0 for 8, and Mitchell Robinson had 4 points and 4 rebounds in his first game against the Knicks team he won a title with. There were 12 lead changes and four ties.

**Injuries:** none reported in the AP game story.

**Up next:** Knicks host the Magic Sunday. Celtics host the Bulls Monday.

## Hawks 128, Hornets 81

**By quarter:** Atlanta 33-35-29-31, Charlotte 20-23-19-19. The Hawks led 68-43 at the half and by as many as 49.

**Top performers:**
- Jalen Johnson (ATL): 19 points, 9-16 FG, 7 rebounds, 8 assists, 3 steals
- Aaron Wiggins (ATL): 19 points off the bench, 7-9 FG
- Naz Reid (CHA): 17 points, 6 rebounds, 7 turnovers
- Nickeil Alexander-Walker (ATL): 14 points
- C.J. McCollum (ATL): 13 points
- Dyson Daniels (ATL): 11 points, 9 rebounds, 7 steals

**Key moments:** the 47-point margin is the largest win in Hawks history, topping a 46-point win over Sacramento in 2017 (AP). Atlanta forced 14 first-half turnovers and Charlotte finished with 27, and all five Hawks starters scored in double figures. The sellout crowd booed the Hornets off at halftime in their first home game since trading LaMelo Ball. Brandon Miller had 6 points on 2-of-14 shooting, 1 for 11 from 3.

**Injuries:** Charlotte again played without Kon Knueppel (hamstring), Coby White (calf) and Grant Williams (hamstring); coach Charles Lee gave no timetable but said all three are progressing (AP).

**Up next:** Hawks host the Rockets tonight. Hornets host the Heat this afternoon.
