## Hawks 132, Magic 125

**By quarter:** Atlanta 28-44-27-33, Orlando 33-29-38-25. The Hawks won the second quarter 44-29 behind two 12-0 runs.

**Top performers:**
- Paolo Banchero (ORL): 35 points, 10-12 FG, 12-16 FT, 10 assists, 7 rebounds
- Nickeil Alexander-Walker (ATL): 31 points, 8-15 FG, 5-8 3PT, 10-13 FT
- Jalen Johnson (ATL): 20 points, 14 rebounds, 6 assists
- Dyson Daniels (ATL): 20 points, 8-13 FG, 5 steals
- Wendell Carter Jr. (ORL): 22 points, 8-10 FG, 7 rebounds
- Franz Wagner (ORL): 22 points, 5 assists

**Key moments:** Banchero's three-point play capped an 11-0 run and gave Orlando a 98-95 lead late in the third. With 4:27 left, Johnson appeared to foul out on an offensive foul, but Quin Snyder's challenge overturned it, and Atlanta never trailed again. Alexander-Walker's back-to-back 3s, the second a 25-footer, made it 128-123 with 51 seconds left. It was Sean Sweeney's first game as Magic coach, and Orlando has now lost six straight to Atlanta (AP).

**Injuries:** no in-game injuries were reported in the AP game story.

**Up next:** Hawks visit the Hornets Friday. Magic host the Timberwolves Friday.

## Lakers 127, Warriors 105

**By quarter:** Golden State 33-19-16-37, Los Angeles 34-32-36-25. The Lakers won the third quarter 36-16.

**Top performers:**
- Luka Doncic (LAL): 28 points in three quarters, 5-12 3PT, 9-9 FT, 8 rebounds, 7 assists, 3 steals
- Walker Kessler (LAL): 26 points, 12-12 FG, 9 rebounds in his Lakers debut

- Stephen Curry (GS): 23 points, 7-15 FG, 7-7 FT in 21 minutes
- Brandon Williams (GS): 14 points off the bench
- Yaxel Lendeborg (GS): 12 points in his NBA debut

**Key moments:** Kessler dunked a Doncic lob on the first possession and never missed. Per the AP, his 12-for-12 night trails only Wilt Chamberlain's 14 for 14 in March 1969 in Lakers history. Doncic scored 23 in the first half during a running exchange with Lendeborg, who drew a technical, and banked in a half-court shot at the buzzer. It was the Lakers' first game since LeBron James left for Philadelphia.

**Injuries:** Jake LaRavia (10 points) left limping in the fourth quarter. Golden State was without Brandin Podziemski (back), Jimmy Butler III, Kristaps Porzingis and Moses Moody. The Lakers were without Collin Sexton (calf) and Cameron Carr (oblique).
