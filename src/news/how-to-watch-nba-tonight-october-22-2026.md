## Cavaliers at 76ers: 7pm ET (4pm PT), ESPN

**Where to watch:** ESPN and the ESPN app. Xfinity Mobile Arena, Philadelphia.

**Local homes when not national:** the [Cavaliers](/teams/cavaliers/) moved to Rock Entertainment Sports Network and Cavs on DAZN, with 15 games free on WOIO 19 and WUAB 43. The [76ers](/teams/76ers/) are on NBC Sports Philadelphia, with its Peacock regional add-on.

**Why watch:** it's the Sixers' home opener and LeBron James' first home game in Philadelphia, against the franchise he started with. Philadelphia beat the Knicks 106-100 Tuesday behind 28 from Tyrese Maxey. Cleveland went 52-30 last season and opens with Donovan Mitchell, James Harden and Evan Mobley.

**Injury report (10am ET):** Cleveland's Jarrett Allen is questionable (right patellar tendinopathy); Khalifa Diop and Nae'Qwan Tomlin are out. Philadelphia has Dominick Barlow, Tacko Fall and Ariel Hukporti out, with Dean Wade questionable (left ankle sprain).

## Nuggets at Thunder: 9:30pm ET (6:30pm PT), ESPN

**Where to watch:** ESPN and the ESPN app. Paycom Center, Oklahoma City.

**Local homes when not national:** the [Nuggets](/teams/nuggets/) are on Altitude Sports and Altitude+; select games on KUSA 9 are **unconfirmed** (one source). The [Thunder](/teams/thunder/) moved to the Thunder Gameday Network (KSBI 52 and KQCW 19) and Thunder+, with 10 games free over the air.

**Why watch:** two of the West's best from last season (Thunder 64-18, Nuggets 54-28). Shai Gilgeous-Alexander had 32 points, 3 steals and 3 blocks in Tuesday's win over the Spurs, and Nikola Jokic and Jamal Murray open Denver's season.

**Injury report (10am ET):** Denver's Julian Strawther is out (left knee sprain) and Spencer Jones is questionable. Oklahoma City has Otega Oweh, Thomas Sorber and Nikola Topic out.
