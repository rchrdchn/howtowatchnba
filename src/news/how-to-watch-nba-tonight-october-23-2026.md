## How can I stream tonight's games?

**Prime Video games:** included with Amazon Prime, $14.99 a month or $139 a year (checked October 18, 2026). A standalone Prime Video membership is $8.99 a month, but Amazon's NBA help page names Prime members.

**Local games:** each team's own stream works in its home market (Bucks+, MavsTV, Monumental+, Pelicans+, Jazz+, SCHN+ and the DAZN team channels, among others). Out of market, all 10 local games are on NBA League Pass, $16.99 a month or $109.99 for the season (checked October 18, 2026).

Want the season total for your team? Try the [cheapest way to watch your team](/tools/cheapest-way-to-watch-your-team/) calculator.

## Will tonight's games be blacked out?

The two Prime Video games are national exclusives: blacked out live on League Pass everywhere in the US, and the four teams' local channels don't carry them.

For the other 10, League Pass blacks out the game only inside either team's home market, where it airs on the local channel instead. Check your ZIP code on the [League Pass blackout checker](/blackouts/).

## Bulls at Bucks: 4pm ET (1pm PT)

**Where to watch:** [Bulls](/teams/bulls/) on Chicago Sports Network and the CHSN app. [Bucks](/teams/bucks/) on My24, free over the air, and Bucks+. Fiserv Forum, Milwaukee.

**Why watch:** an afternoon tip and the Bucks' home opener. Both teams lost on Wednesday: Milwaukee fell 128-116 at Washington, and Chicago lost 135-92 at Toronto with 27 turnovers.

**Injury report (10am ET):** Chicago has Nic Claxton out, Jordan Hawkins and Dailyn Swain questionable (illness), and Tobe Awaka, Zach Collins and Josh Giddey probable. Milwaukee has no one listed.

## Knicks at Celtics: 7pm ET (4pm PT), Prime Video

**Where to watch:** Prime Video. TD Garden, Boston.

**Local homes when not national:** the [Knicks](/teams/knicks/) are on MSG Network. The [Celtics](/teams/celtics/) are on NBC Sports Boston; its Peacock regional add-on is **unconfirmed** (one source).

**Why watch:** the defending champion Knicks and the Celtics are both 0-1 and meet in Boston's home opener. New York lost 106-100 to the 76ers, and Boston lost 121-113 at Detroit despite 38 from Paul George.

**Injury report (10am ET):** Boston's Devin Carter is out (illness). New York lists only two-way players.
