## 76ers 114, Cavaliers 106

**By quarter:** Cleveland 35-34-21-16, Philadelphia 18-27-37-32. The Cavs led 69-45 at the half; the Sixers won the second half 69-37.

**Top performers:**
- Tyrese Maxey (PHI): 32 points, 12-23 FG, 6-8 FT, 6 assists
- Donovan Mitchell (CLE): 32 points, 11-19 FG, 8-11 FT, 10 rebounds
- Joel Embiid (PHI): 24 points, 8-16 FG, 7-9 FT, 7 rebounds
- Jaylen Brown (PHI): 21 points, 10-20 FG, 8 rebounds
- Evan Mobley (CLE): 18 points, 7-13 FG, 8 rebounds
- LeBron James (PHI): 17 points, 10 rebounds, 5 assists, 3 steals
- James Harden (CLE): 16 points, 5 assists, 6 turnovers

**Key moments:** Mitchell had 22 in the first half as Cleveland built a 29-point lead, and the home crowd booed late in the second quarter after Philadelphia missed 11 of 15 3s with 14 turnovers. Embiid scored seven straight in the fourth to make it 96-95, and Maxey keyed a 15-0 fourth-quarter run that put the Sixers ahead for good. Maxey and Harden went nose to nose in the fourth before they were separated. Per the AP, it was the biggest comeback win of James' career, in his first home game in Philadelphia. Cleveland committed 21 turnovers.

**Injuries:** no in-game injuries were reported in the AP game story. Jarrett Allen, listed as questionable, started and had 14 points.

**Up next:** Cavaliers host the Nets in their home opener Saturday. 76ers host the Bucks Saturday (NBA TV) and the Pistons Sunday.

## Thunder 96, Nuggets 84

**By quarter:** Denver 27-21-16-20, Oklahoma City 24-20-26-26. The Thunder held Denver to 36 points after halftime.

**Top performers:**
- Jalen Williams (OKC): 21 points, 7-15 FG, 5 rebounds
- Aaron Gordon (DEN): 20 points, 6-9 FG, 3-4 3PT
- Shai Gilgeous-Alexander (OKC): 17 points, 6-19 FG, 3-11 3PT
- Chet Holmgren (OKC): 12 points, 17 rebounds, 4 blocks
- Ajay Mitchell (OKC): 12 points, 5 steals off the bench
- Nikola Jokic (DEN): 13 points, 5-19 FG, 7 rebounds, 5 assists
- DeMar DeRozan (DEN): 13 points off the bench

**Key moments:** Gilgeous-Alexander missed all seven of his first-half shots as Denver led by as many as nine, and per the AP he finished under 20 points for the first time in 141 games. A Mitchell layup gave Oklahoma City a 64-62 lead, its first since 3-0, and the Thunder led 70-64 after three. A Gilgeous-Alexander 3 made it 93-76 with 1:44 left. Denver shot 38% and turned it over 22 times, and Jamal Murray scored 6 on 2-of-11 shooting.

**Injuries:** no in-game injuries were reported in the AP game story.

**Up next:** Nuggets host the Pelicans Saturday. Thunder host the Clippers Sunday.
