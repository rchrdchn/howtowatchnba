## Pistons 121, Celtics 113

**By quarter:** Boston 28-30-30-25, Detroit 27-18-37-39. Detroit won the fourth quarter by 14.

**Top performers:**
- Paul George (BOS): 38 points, 12-21 FG, 7-12 3PT, 7-8 FT, 5 rebounds, 5 steals
- Cade Cunningham (DET): 22 points, 8-18 FG, 8 assists, 4 rebounds
- Jalen Duren (DET): 22 points, 8-13 FG, 9 rebounds
- Duncan Robinson (DET): 21 points, 5-9 3PT
- Javonte Green (DET): 19 points, 5-8 3PT, all after halftime
- Jayson Tatum (BOS): 13 points, 6-23 FG, 0-7 3PT, 11 assists

**Key moments:** George hit three 3-pointers in under a minute early in the third quarter to push Boston's lead to 72-50. Detroit cut it to six by the end of the third, then took over: Green's go-ahead 3, a Duren three-point play and a Robinson 3 made it 107-99 with 5:10 left. Green's fifth 3 put Detroit up 13 with 3:33 to go. George's 38 is the most by a Celtic in his team debut (AP).

**Injuries:** no in-game injuries were reported in the AP game story.

**Up next:** Celtics host the Knicks Friday on Prime Video. Pistons visit the Heat Friday.

## 76ers 106, Knicks 100

**By quarter:** Philadelphia 23-31-29-23, New York 25-33-18-24. The Sixers took control with a 29-18 third quarter.

**Top performers:**
- Tyrese Maxey (PHI): 28 points, 9-19 FG, 8-9 FT, 5 assists, 3 steals, 3 blocks
- Jalen Brunson (NY): 21 points, 6-19 FG, 8-8 FT
- Jaylen Brown (PHI): 19 points, 8 rebounds, 6 assists in his 76ers debut
- Karl-Anthony Towns (NY): 19 points, 12 rebounds, 3-4 3PT
- LeBron James (PHI): 14 points, 9 rebounds, 7 assists to open his 24th season
- Joel Embiid (PHI): 13 points, 11 rebounds, 6 assists, 3 blocks

**Key moments:** the Knicks raised their championship banner and got their rings before tip. Philadelphia closed the third on an 11-0 run, capped by Brown's buzzer-beating 3 for an 83-76 lead. New York got within 96-95 on a Towns 3, but Embiid answered with a 3 and the Sixers held on. The Knicks shot 37% from the field.

**Injuries:** no in-game injuries were reported in the AP game story.

**Up next:** Sixers host the Cavaliers Thursday on ESPN. Knicks visit the Celtics Friday on Prime Video.

## Thunder 116, Spurs 104

**By quarter:** Oklahoma City 34-31-28-23, San Antonio 28-16-36-24. OKC led by 21 at the half and for all but the first five minutes.

**Top performers:**
- Shai Gilgeous-Alexander (OKC): 32 points, 13-27 FG, 6 rebounds, 4 assists, 3 steals, 3 blocks
- Stephon Castle (SA): 24 points, 5-12 3PT, 7 rebounds
- Jared McCain (OKC): 20 points, 7-9 FG, 6-8 3PT off the bench
- Dylan Harper (SA): 18 points, 7 rebounds, 5 assists
- Chet Holmgren (OKC): 14 points, 7 rebounds, 4 blocks
- Victor Wembanyama (SA): 12 points, 1-13 FG, 10-12 FT, 13 rebounds, 6 blocks

**Key moments:** early in the second quarter, De'Aaron Fox got a Flagrant 1 for throwing Chet Holmgren to the floor, and the scrum that followed drew technicals for Wembanyama, Jaylin Williams, Jalen Williams and Kenrich Williams. OKC answered with a 9-0 run and later a 13-0 run for a 59-33 lead. San Antonio opened the second half on a 23-9 run to get within single digits, but Gilgeous-Alexander and McCain combined for 25 second-half points. Wembanyama missed his first 12 shots; his only field goal was an alley-oop at the third-quarter buzzer.

**Injuries:** no in-game injuries were reported in the AP game story.

**Up next:** Thunder host the Nuggets Thursday on ESPN. Spurs host the Rockets in Austin Friday on Prime Video.
