## How can I stream tonight's games?

Peacock streams all three games. Peacock Premium is $12.99 a month with ads, or $129.99 a year (prices checked October 18, 2026).

NBC is also free over the air with an antenna, and it's on cable and live TV streaming services that carry your local NBC station.

## Will tonight's games be blacked out?

On League Pass, yes. National NBC and Peacock games are blacked out live on League Pass across the US, so League Pass is not an option tonight.

Your team's usual local channel will not air these games either. Check any ZIP code on the [League Pass blackout checker](/blackouts/) for regular-season local games.

## Celtics at Pistons: 3pm ET, NBC

**Where to watch:** NBC and Peacock. Little Caesars Arena, Detroit.

**Local homes when not national:** the Celtics are on [NBC Sports Boston](/teams/celtics/) (its Peacock regional add-on is **unconfirmed**, one source so far). The Pistons moved off FanDuel Sports Network to [WMYD TV20, free over the air, and DAZN](/teams/pistons/).

**Why watch:** last season's top two seeds in the East meet in the first game of the year. Cade Cunningham and the Pistons host Jayson Tatum, Paul George and a retooled Celtics roster (NBA.com).

**Injury report (9am ET):** Boston's Devin Carter is out with an illness. Detroit's Taurean Prince is questionable for personal reasons.

## 76ers at Knicks: 7pm ET, NBC

**Where to watch:** NBC and Peacock. Madison Square Garden, New York.

**Local homes when not national:** the 76ers are on [NBC Sports Philadelphia](/teams/76ers/). The Knicks are on [MSG Network](/teams/knicks/), with MSG+ on DAZN in market.

**Why watch:** the defending champion Knicks start their title defense, and LeBron James and Jaylen Brown make their Sixers debuts (NBA.com).

**Injury report (9am ET):** Philadelphia lists Anfernee Simons (left hamstring), Adem Bona (left foot) and Dean Wade (left ankle) as questionable. Dominick Barlow, Tacko Fall and Ariel Hukporti are out. The Knicks have no one listed.

## Thunder at Spurs: 9:30pm ET, NBC

**Where to watch:** NBC and Peacock. Frost Bank Center, San Antonio.

**Local homes when not national:** the Thunder moved off FanDuel Sports Network to [Thunder+ and KSBI 52](/teams/thunder/). The Spurs stream local games on [DAZN](/teams/spurs/); the free-TV station for their 22 promised over-the-air games is **unconfirmed** (not named yet).

**Why watch:** it's a rematch of last season's seven-game West Finals, which San Antonio won. Two-time reigning MVP Shai Gilgeous-Alexander faces reigning Defensive Player of the Year Victor Wembanyama (NBA.com).

**Injury report (9am ET):** Oklahoma City has Otega Oweh, Thomas Sorber and Nikola Topic out. San Antonio's Tobias Harris is out with a left calf strain.
